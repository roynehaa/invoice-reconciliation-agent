    How the real agent runs

    n8n workflow: invoice by email or upload → Gemini reads the PDF → rules check it against a Google Sheets PO list → every result is logged, and flagged invoices post to a Slack channel for finance.

    Tested result

    20 fictional invoices across 3 layouts, 9 with planted errors, including one scanned image. 20/20 correct decisions. When the AI service was overloaded, those invoices were sent to a person, never auto-approved.
